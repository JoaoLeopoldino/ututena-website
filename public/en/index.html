FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Keeping the essentials
simple.

Can't find your case? We are happy to understand your organisation's requirements.

What artificial intelligence features are available?+

AI-assisted course authoring from manuals and procedures, a virtual mentor by text and voice, conversation simulations and AI-assisted analysis of open answers. They are optional, enabled per module, with separate usage costs and human validation preserved.

Is company data sent to AI services?+

AI services that process personal data are disabled by default. When enabled under a contractual authorisation, the necessary data goes only to the configured model provider, under the conditions agreed with your organisation.

Where is the data hosted?+

By default, on infrastructure managed by Utu Lab in the European Union, with per-organisation isolation, private files and daily backups. Dedicated hosting or another location is available on request.

How is data protection handled?+

The customer organisation is the data controller and Utu Lab acts as processor. Before any real data is used, a data processing agreement is signed and the applicable formalities under Angola's Law No. 22/11 are prepared together with the Data Protection Agency (APD).

Is Ututena a platform or a service?+

Both. Ututena is Utu Lab's platform, built on the open source Mentingo core (MIT licence) and extended with its own modules for skills, certificates, integrations and limited connectivity. It includes implementation, hosting, support and continuous improvement, with no separate software licence fee.

We already have an LMS. Can we migrate?+

Yes. We migrate users, organisational structure, courses, enrolments, history, certificates and skills — for example from Totara — with a pilot migration, a reconciliation report and sign-off by your team. In some cases, integrating or evolving your current platform is a better fit than replacing it.

Can it work with limited internet access?+

Yes, within clear limits: adaptive streaming video for low bandwidth and an offline mode for text, PDF and quizzes, synchronising when the connection returns. Behaviour on Android phones and real networks is validated in each project.

Can it integrate with our HR or ERP system?+

Yes. We have connectors for Primavera ERP (employees, managers, roles and business units), Microsoft 365 and Teams, plus data export to Power BI. Other systems connect via API or CSV/XLSX import. Scope and access are defined during discovery.

What language is the platform in?+

The interface is available in English and Angolan Portuguese, as well as Spanish, French, German, Polish and Czech, so each user can work in their own language. Support is provided in Portuguese and English.

Can certificates be shared on LinkedIn?+

Yes. Each issued certificate has a public verification page and employees can add it to their LinkedIn profile, with your organisation shown as the issuer. Certificates can have expiry dates, be revoked and be renewed automatically.

Which content formats are supported?+

A native course editor, PDF, video and SCORM 1.2 packages. SCORM 2004 and xAPI are not included out of the box.

What support is included?+

Support in English and Portuguese from a team in Angola, on business days from 08:30 to 17:30 (Angola time), with response times by priority — within 1 business hour for critical incidents — a monthly service report and a quarterly review. 24/7 coverage is optional.

How long does implementation take?+

An enterprise pilot runs for 30 days, with up to 100 participants and one use case. A full implementation, with migration and integrations, typically takes 12 to 16 weeks.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing is by proposal. The model combines a one-off implementation, paid against acceptance milestones, with a monthly managed service fee that covers hosting, maintenance, backups and support. The amount depends on the number of users, migration, integrations and customisation.

What if we want to end the contract?+

Your organisation's data, content and records belong to you. At the end of the contract they are exported in documented formats, with transition support.
