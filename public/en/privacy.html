PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your data is processed for a clear purpose.

This page describes the processing associated with discovery and contact requests made through the Ututena website. The Portuguese version (Privacidade) prevails in case of discrepancy.

Who receives the data

UTU LAB – COMÉRCIO E SERVIÇOS (SU), LDA., Luanda, Angola. Contact: info@ututena.com.

Data collected and purpose

The form asks for your name, work email, organisation, need and, optionally, a message. Technical campaign source parameters may also be collected. This data is used to respond to your request, analyse your requirements and contact your organisation about the opportunity identified.

Processing and providers

Requests are transmitted through Cloudflare infrastructure, with Cloudflare Turnstile anti-abuse protection, and sent by email through a transactional email provider configured by Utu Lab. This form does not automatically create an account or add the visitor to a marketing list.

Usage analytics

To understand how the page is used, we use PostHog, with data hosted in the European Union. We record pages visited and interactions such as clicks on contact buttons, navigation in the demo and starting or submitting the form. The values typed into the form are never sent to analytics.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits and loading performance in aggregate, without cookies or personal identifiers.

Without your permission, analytics only work during your visit, with no cookies or browser storage. If you accept in the notice shown on the page, we store an identifier in your browser to recognise returning visits and record browsing sessions with form field content hidden. Your choice is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time through the «Cookies» link in the footer of the home page.

Retention and rights

Request data is kept for a maximum of 6 months from receipt and then deleted, unless longer retention is required by applicable legal obligations. You can request information, correction or deletion of your data at info@ututena.com, subject to the relevant legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions: info@ututena.com.
